01 — Racing / Pets

Pet Races

collect and upgrade pets, race them on short tracks against other players' pets, and earn coins for cosmetics.

Pet Collector Racing Idle Grind

Why this wins

Roblox signals

  • Adopt Me 531K CCU — pet collecting is the platform's #1 proven loop
  • Pet Sim 99 — idle pet grind holds CCU for years
  • No pet racing game exists in the top 100
  • Short 60-second races = max shareable clip length

Why it's easy to build

  • Linear track — no open world needed
  • Pets are stat blocks + models, not complex AI
  • Race logic = simple speed + RNG modifier calculation
  • Full game scope achievable in 4–6 weeks solo

Core pillars

Hatch & collect

Egg hatching with rarity tiers. Common to Mythic pets. Each pet has 3 stats: speed, stamina, luck. Duplicates merge into stronger versions.

Race live

8-player races, 60 seconds. Your pet runs automatically — stats determine outcome with small RNG variance. Spectate others. Replay last race.

Earn & upgrade

Win coins, spend on food items that boost stats temporarily. Cosmetic trails and race auras sold for Robux. Seasonal tournament with exclusive egg prize.

02 — PvP / Ranked

Blade Duel

1v1 sword fighting with a small move set, big skill gap, and a ranked ladder. think classic roblox sword fight but with modern ranked seasons and cosmetics.

1v1 PvP Ranked Skill-Based

Why this wins

Roblox signals

  • Classic Sword Fight on the Heights — ancient game, still 5K+ CCU passively
  • BedWars proves ranked PvP with cosmetics is a top-10 CCU formula
  • 1v1 format = no matchmaking wait, instant session start
  • Skill gap = competitive players never leave — they chase rank forever

Why it's easy to build

  • Sword combat = hitbox check + damage value. Roblox's most-understood system.
  • Arena is a single small map — 30 minute build
  • Ranked = win/loss ELO calculation, one script
  • 3-move kit means no complex ability system needed at launch

Core pillars

Fight clean

3-move kit: slash, block, dash. Simple enough to learn in 10 seconds, deep enough to master over 100 hours. Block timing creates a skill gap no amount of grinding can bypass.

Climb ranked

ELO-based 1v1 matchmaking. 6 rank tiers — Bronze to Legend. Season resets keep the ladder competitive. Top 10 Legend players get a nameplate visible server-wide.

Flex cosmetics

Blade skins, kill effects, idle animations — all cosmetic, Robux only. Rank-exclusive blade effect for each tier. Season-end exclusive skin for top 100. Zero P2W.
